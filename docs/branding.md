# Renowa Labs Brand System

## Brand Overview
Renowa Labs is a small technology and product lab from Azerbaijan. The brand should feel calm, sharp, technical, and product-driven. It should make the team feel real, focused, and capable without pretending to be a large corporation.

## Mission
Build intelligent software products that solve focused problems for local and global markets.

## Positioning
AI-first products, built from Azerbaijan for the world.

Core Azerbaijani sentence:
"Biz Renowa Labs olaraq yerli ve qlobal bazar ucun texnoloji, esasen Suni Intellekt agirliqli heller uzerinde calisiriq."

Use the Azerbaijani sentence with native characters in product and brand contexts where rendering is confirmed:
"Biz Renowa Labs olaraq yerli və qlobal bazar üçün texnoloji, əsasən Süni İntellekt ağırlıqlı həllər üzərində çalışırıq."

## Target Audience
Renowa Labs speaks to product users, collaborators, partners, technical operators, and people who value useful software over noisy marketing.

## Brand Personality
- Small team, high standards.
- Calm, technical, direct.
- Curious about applied AI, but grounded in real products.
- Polished without feeling inflated.

## Tone Of Voice
Use short, confident, human sentences. Avoid inflated claims, empty buzzwords, and broad corporate language.

Good examples:
- Small team. Sharp products.
- Built for real users, not just demos.
- We turn focused problems into useful software.
- Designed, built, and shipped by a small product lab.

Avoid:
- Generic transformation language.
- Cyberpunk AI language.
- Overstated global-leader claims.
- Robot, brain, and circuit metaphors.

## Messaging Examples
Homepage line:
"AI-first products, built from Azerbaijan for the world."

Supporting line:
"Renowa Labs is a small technology lab creating intelligent software for local and global markets."

Product page intro:
"We design, build, and ship focused software products, from mobile apps to AI-assisted tools."

## Homepage Copy
The homepage should act as a portal, not a long landing page. Keep it close to one viewport:
- Renowa Labs wordmark.
- Main positioning line.
- One short supporting line.
- Products, About, and Contact links.
- Small product shortcuts.

## Product Page Copy Style
Product cards should name the product, state the value in one line, and show category, platform, status, and a clear App Store action. Product detail pages can expand into features and product context, but should stay concise.

## Color System
Use CSS variables:
- `--background`: obsidian green-black.
- `--foreground`: off-white.
- `--surface`: slightly lifted dark surface.
- `--surface-elevated`: focused panel surface.
- `--muted`: quiet dark field.
- `--muted-foreground`: blue-gray text.
- `--border`: translucent line.
- `--accent`: warm off-white gray.
- `--accent-soft`: soft accent wash.
- `--glow`: restrained monochrome lift.

Accent should come from contrast, spacing, and type, not color. Do not make the site feel like a neon AI template.

## Typography Rules
Use Geist or a modern system sans-serif. Keep hierarchy clear:
- Large confident homepage headline.
- Short body copy.
- Comfortable mobile line lengths.
- No tiny unreadable metadata.
- No crowded sections.

## Logo And Wordmark Direction
The symbol is based on a precise R-like product mark with a signal endpoint. It should feel like a lab stamp, not a mascot. The wordmark should be text-first and clear at small sizes.

## SVG Asset Direction
SVGs should be original, lightweight, and restrained:
- Subtle terminal fields and quiet grid traces.
- Thin lines, small nodes, minimal gradients.
- No huge DOM trees.
- No heavy filters.
- No obvious flag imagery.

## Layout Principles
- Homepage stays close to `100svh`.
- Inner pages use generous spacing and readable sections.
- Product grids are responsive and do not depend on horizontal scroll.
- Cards are individual repeated items only.
- Avoid nested cards and decorative page-section panels.

## Motion Principles
Motion should be subtle and useful:
- Gentle text reveal.
- Low-amplitude SVG line movement.
- Hover states that feel precise.
- Product card reveal on entry.
- Respect reduced motion globally.
- Animate transform and opacity, not layout.

## Product Card Rules
Each product card should include:
- App-icon area.
- Name.
- One-line value proposition.
- Category and status chips.
- App Store action.
- View product link.
- Calm hover motion.

## Accessibility Rules
- Use semantic HTML and heading order.
- Keep visible focus states.
- Use `aria-current` on active navigation.
- Make mobile menu keyboard accessible.
- Keep good contrast.
- Do not rely on animation to communicate meaning.

## SEO Rules
- Each route must have real HTML content.
- Use Next.js metadata per page.
- Add canonical URLs, Open Graph, Twitter cards, sitemap, and robots.
- Product detail metadata should come from product data.

## Do And Don't Examples
Do:
- "Small team. Sharp products."
- "AI-first products, built from Azerbaijan for the world."
- "Built for real users, not just demos."

Don't:
- "Unlock the future with next-generation AI synergy."
- "World-class cyber innovation collective."
- "Robot-powered brain platform for everything."

## Future Brand Expansion Notes
As real products mature, replace placeholder product data with App Store links, screenshots, product icons, and tighter product descriptions. Add case-study pages only when there is enough real content to justify them.
