# GPT Image Asset Prompts

Use these prompts to create future Renowa Labs bitmap assets. Keep results calm, technical, minimal, and suitable for a refined software lab. Avoid generic robots, brains, circuit boards, overused neon cyberpunk styling, and obvious flag imagery.

## Abstract Renowa Labs Hero Visual
Create an abstract hero visual for Renowa Labs, a small AI-first software product lab . Use a flat charcoal background, monochrome terminal-like marks, quiet geometric structure, and soft depth. The image should feel calm, technical, intelligent, and product-driven. No robots, no brain icons, no circuit board patterns, no bright glow.

## App Store Product Showcase Mockup
Create a polished App Store product showcase mockup for a small software lab. Show three iPhone-style app screens with minimal UI, dark charcoal surfaces, grayscale accents, and clear product-focused composition. Keep the screens generic and replaceable. Avoid fake App Store badges, brand names from other companies, robots, brains, and loud marketing visuals.

## Monochrome Team Portrait Style
Create a monochrome portrait style for a three-person technology lab team. Use understated studio lighting, calm expressions, sharp silhouettes, and a technical editorial feel. Keep the result realistic, human, and modest. Avoid cartoon avatars, anonymous stock-photo energy, corporate boardroom styling, and exaggerated futurism.

## Open Graph Image
Create a 1200 by 630 Open Graph image for Renowa Labs. Include a calm charcoal background, subtle terminal-grid geometry, a clear central text area for "Renowa Labs", and restrained grayscale contrast. The image should look high-quality, modern, and readable in social previews. Avoid busy details, robots, brains, and neon cyberpunk.

## Product Thumbnail Background
Create a reusable product thumbnail background for AI-first mobile products. Use layered dark surfaces, minimal app-window geometry, small signal marks, and soft accent lines. Leave safe empty space for product names and icons. Avoid photoreal devices unless requested, and avoid generic AI symbols.

## Subtle AI Lab Texture
Create a subtle AI lab texture for website backgrounds. Use quiet grid alignment, faint terminal marks, small nodes, and flat charcoal tones with restrained gray highlights. The texture must be low contrast and should not compete with text. Avoid circuits, brains, robots, particles, and bright glowing blobs.
