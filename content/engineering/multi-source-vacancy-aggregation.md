This article is being developed as a detailed account of the product and engineering boundaries required to bring vacancies from multiple trusted sources into one candidate experience.

The final version will cover source-specific collection contracts, canonical vacancy records, provenance, freshness, retry behaviour, respectful request policies, change detection, and the separation between internal collection mechanisms and the unified Hirinq product presented to candidates.

It will remain excluded from indexing until the technical examples, operational trade-offs, and failure-recovery guidance have received a full engineering review.
