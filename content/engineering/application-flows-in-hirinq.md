Vacancy sources use different application methods. Some employers can accept a direct submission, some ask candidates to send an email, and others require an external form. Hirinq needs to make each route understandable before the candidate starts and preserve useful activity state when the flow leaves the product.

The completed article will examine clear application labels, email handoff, attachment expectations, external-link context, direct form accessibility, activity tracking boundaries, privacy, and the difference between opening an application and knowing that an employer received it.

This draft is intentionally non-indexable until the final interaction examples and privacy review are complete.
